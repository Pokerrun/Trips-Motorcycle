Dreher Island State Park, SC Motorcycle Trip 9-21-20   4 nights
 
Bill used to live there and planned trip.  We are tagging along.  Left Monday, returned on Friday.
 
Cost:          Food:                      147
                   Gas                                86
         Hotel⁄Camping         104   Tent camping except on return night, it was raining and stayed in Days Inn.
                                    -----
         TOTAL                  337
 
444 miles. 
Met at Falmouth
Rt27 to 68 to I75
I75 to Corbin Ky
Rt 25E to I40
I40 to 26E to Dreher Island State Park
  
Mon  Beautiful warm sunny day.  11:00 meet at McDonalds Falmouth.  Overshot AA turnoff by 10 miles and had to backtrack.  1⁄2 hour late!!!!!    Rode all day to KOA campsite just off I40.  Real close to I40, truck noise loud.  Shitty camp site.  Cold, but warm in sleeping bag with great new air matress, very comfortable.
 
 
 
Tues  Rode to Dreher Island, SC.  Rogers lens dropped out of glasses when we were almost there.  Switched to contacts.  Sub sandwhich picked up for dinner.  Overcharged me, clerk all confused.  Got to camp at 8PM.  Bill able to pull screen out and crawl into cabin, key hanging from railing.  Roger and I were able to setup tents in light.
 
 
Wed  Went to oldtime hardware store in Newport.  Old guy whoses father won Nobel Physics prize and brother about to do same talked heavily to Roger, since he has nuclear interest.  Gave Roger a CD on fathers nuclear talk.  Ate outside on Main Street cafe.  Lots of old junk!  I needed chain tightened, adjustable wrenches and vice grips kept slipping off.  Luckily Bill had the wrench.  Tighted to full travel, but still loose.  Went to walmart, Bill got baggy ass rain suit for $13.  Showed us his house on lake he used to live in.  Showed us Resturant on lake.  Roger and I went to resturant and had crappy fish & chips dinner.
Took beer and walked to Bills cabin, sat around fire.
 
Thurs.  Left for home.   26W  to 23W, to Portsmouth, to Rt 52W, to Rt 68 and cross Ohio, Rt8 to home.
 
Looks like rain. After 1 hour put rainsuits on and it rained.  Stopped at nice gas station⁄resturant and had sandwhiches⁄coffee.  Traveled until 5PM.
Days Inn at $71⁄night with AAA discount.
Ordered Pizza delivery and Roger surprised Bill and I with beer run across highway.  Bills Bday yesterday, so he was surprised with beer present.
Fri.  Almost left jacket in room.  After I'm all suited up with rain gear outside, realized frikin jacket is still hanging in room closet!!!
Ride home in rain.  Rain quit after 2 hours of foggy⁄wet roads. 23N to Portsmouth.   My bike fellover at a stop, when slightly pushed by me.  Guess not much weight on stand, no damage.  Lunch at Buckeye Dairy joint and sat on picknic tables.  Easy ride home.  I am beat!
 
41055 odometer at end   1161 miles.
 
GREAT TRIP!
