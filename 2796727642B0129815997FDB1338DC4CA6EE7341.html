Mohican State Park, Oh   9-27-26   Roger & I, 1 night
 
9-27-26 Sun  Met at Leesburg Dairy Barn at 10AM, on Rt 28.  Had breakfast and left.
- Roger wanted to stop at Mohawk Dam.   Stopped and told me about him and his father going through the earthen dam chutes with a            
  canoe. It's maybe 150 yards long being an earthen dam.  Risky but they really couldn't walk around it with all their gear.
- Stayed at a Sherman Cabin instead of tenting.  Only $70 + fees and 10% off with his Golden Buckeye card.
  Small but new, no running water or bathroom.  Just a bed, bunkbed, microwave, Refrig, heat⁄air....all you really need.
- Found firewatch platform.  Climbed steps, got a few pics.
- Went into town for pizza and salad.  Picked up firewood, beer and peanuts.
- Built fire.  I ran up to camp store and bought more firewood.
 
9-28-26 7:30 alarm
- McDonalds for breakfast.
- Off to see our old Milacron buddy, Jim Rogers.  Living on Apple Valley Lake, only 20 minutes away. ($1.6 Mil house)
  Jim gave us tour of house.  Sat on his lake deck and had lunch with wife Marsha.
  Very nice visit.
- Back to Leesburg Dairy Barn, where we split off.
- I felt bike acting just a little funny when almost home.  On ferry, 100% rear flat!
  Slowly drove home.  Plugged it next day.
 
470  miles.
gas              50
food             70
Cabins        78 (full amount, Roger bought firewood, beer, peanuts)
Firewood     8
--------------------
               $   206
 
 
Great weather, simple trip, good to see Jim, nice getaway!
Got real lucky with flat so close to home!
