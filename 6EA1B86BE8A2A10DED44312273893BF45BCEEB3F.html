Carr Creek State Park Ky 8-14-24   1 night Motorcycle trip by myself.
 
350  miles.
gas            28
food           30
Campsite   36
firewood    12
--------------------
               $106 
 
On a whim, took off at 3 PM just to get away, after Rotary Treasurer coup. Not sure where to go, but finally decided on this reasonably close destination.
Called and they said someone will be there until 9.  (Online for next day only)
Stopped at Kroger for camp food.
 
Nice 3 hour drive on twisty highways through big hills..
Bought 2 loads of firewood for $12.  Campsite $36
Swam in river beach area.  Only soul around.  Warm water.  Can't swim straight or very long...haha.
Shower and making dinner.  Beans and Kungpao, add water for noodle dish.
Coffee and oatmeal breakfast.
Left at 10AM
Saw a sign about small town museum.  See pic below.  Closed.  60 year old came over and chatted about bikes and town for 10 minutes there.
Stopped at Brooksville courthouse to get auto plate stickers.
Subway before home.
Home at 3.
 
Nice little getaway for 1 night!
