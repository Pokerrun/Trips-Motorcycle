Ozarks⁄Minneapolis⁄Sturgis  9-6-21
 
6 days with Roger and Bill to Ozarks, then I split off on last day and headed to Rockford, Minneapolis, Sturgis, Black Hills, Badlands and backroads back!
5034 miles!   Oil change before leaving and 2 tires on the road.  19 days total.  All tent camping except 2 nights Chuck, 2 Airbnb.
 
9-6-21 Monday.  Met them at McDonad's, Dry Ridge.   Drove to Land Between the Lakes  "Kentucky Dam State Park "and camped.
Roger and I went to Dinner in very confusing lake area with twisty roads.  Couldn't find the darn place coming back in dark.  Cop stopped and told us it's 1 mile straight down road!
Tues Drove throughMissouri to Buffalo Point Campground, Ozarks of Arkansaw.  Camped 3 nights here and road many loops.
Neighbor Mr. Louissianna, "Roper" was a character.  Gave us 2 beers each 1st night , later a flyswatter, Koozy holder, and detergent jug when we got muddy!
Roger and I walked down to the very clean Buffalo River that was way too low for kyacking⁄cannoeing, even though they were selling anyways.
 
 
 
Fri 9-10 We left Ozarks and headed back.  Stayed at same campground, Kentucky Dam State Park .
Sun 9-12  I split off to see family in Rockford, since we're due South of it and Bill wanted to keep going.  They headed to Cumberland Falls before going home.
        Stayed at Chucks.
Tues 9-13 Saw Connie.  Dennis too worried about Covid, stayed home.
                  Washed bike at Chucks.   Got rear tire replaced, cord coming through, which I wasn't aware of.
Wed 9-14  Took off to Minneapolis to see Teresa's family.  Bought front tire in Madison and hauled to another shop who installed it.  Arrived at Teresa's by 7PM.
         She had a very nice Dinner for us, great salad, chicken dish and fruit.  Staying at nice Airbnb couple miles away for 2 nights.
Thurs 9-15  Gave me the tour. George Floyd memorial, where he died, park for lunch.  Teresa and I took a long walk along the creek.  Then she drove us around city.
 
 
Thurs 9-16  left and decided to head for Sturgis, since I was so far North and had time.
Stayed at campsite where it stormed like crazy almost uprooting tent.  Stayed dried though.  Host and other man were surprised tent made it through the storm.
Fri 9-17 Stops along the way before reaching Sturgis campground.   State Tropper stopped me in a town, for speeding but surprsingly wrote me a warning.
 
Stayed at Sturgis campground for 2 nights and rode Blackhills roads.  Saw Crazy Horse and Mt Rushmore.  Wanted $20 to enter Custer State Park, so didn't see.
        
 
Sun 9-19 returning home via backroads.  Rt 14 East through S Dakota.  Got to camp late.  Picked a open spot and decided no tent needed.  Big moon out  and just sleeping under stars in bag, cowboy camping!  Too bad it started raining!  Pulled plastic sheet over bag and probably got 2 hours max sleep.  Didn't pay and left by 7AM.  Rough night!
Mon 9-20 Cutting down into Iowa.  Crystal Lake camping right on lake.  Cop went by and turned around to give me a speeding ticket.  Asked about rush and I told him trying to get to camp before dark and showed him on GPS.  NO TICKET again!!!
 
Tues 9-21  Drove through Rodman and Greene.  Believe I identified house we grew up in. Lunch in Greene bar on Main Street.  Found hill I remebered as a kid with big haunted house on top of hill, but obviouslly it's gone.  Elementary school still there. 
Sue not interested in Moline visit so I passed her and took scenic Great River Road down the Mississippi in Illinois.  Found camp . Blanchard Island, just off road, 2.5 mile gravel road to camp directly on Mississippi.  I was only camper per host and he's leaving too, I'm it... a little erie since noone would hear my screams!!!!
Water in spicket but thats it.  Nice restrooms but just an outhouse w⁄o elec or water!  Perfect night and no Hells Angels came to bother me.
 
Sun Drove down through Quincy, Il.  Stopped at visitor center overlooking river.  Stopped at Copperhead bar along river and had lunch.  Ver yfriendly and she gave me a Koozy as souvenir.  Stayed at Pere Marquette Campground along river.
 
 
Thurs 9-23 Passed St Louis and going to take Rt50 all the way to Cincy.  Couple of historical stops.  Birdhaven says it's  1 mile up road.  I went 2.5 and found a beautiful sanctuary.  Crappy campground found for $20 where he put me in tent section that was a little wet.  Set tent up in dry area but had bought wood in town and found lots of dead limbs so I really was entertained with good campfire into night!
Fri  9-24-21  Tues  Passed through Seymour l and had lunch at a crazy busy cool diner.  Also walked to Magic Books used bookstore and bought 4 books.
And finally back home at 3PM.
 
A little "after gift":  Speeding citation in Waterloo for $75!  
 
GREAT 19  DAY TRIP!!!!!!!!!!!
