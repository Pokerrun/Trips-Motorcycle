Smokies 8-3-23 with Nephew Arik
 
Arik rode his Sportster from Chicago and arrived Thurs at 7PM.
 
Fri, left Augusta at 8;45 AM and arrived at ABNB at 10:30PM.  Rained all the way, but still enjoyable.
 
Saturday, did the whole Smokies circle.
Sunday, long drive back.
 
976 total miles for me.  Arik has another 700!  He's young and tough!
 
 
On the way down we ate lunch at the historic Boone Tavern Hotel in Bera Ky, for a reasonable price.
 
 
Arik arranged an ABNB for 2 nights.  We got the whole lower floor .  Great view and fire pit ,with free wood.
 
 
Saturday, touring the Smokies.                                             Deals Gap Resturant⁄Tourist stop after doing the Dragon!
 
 
Full day, including downtown Gatlinburg traffic jam, Blue Ridge Parkway for 5 minutes, Cherohala Skyway and the Dragon twice!  Arik did really well on Dragon considering it was his first time!
 
 
 
 
Blue Ridge Parkway and then on the way back to the ABNB, we did the Dragon again.
Finally home and treated ourself to a Gourmet meal at the Beehive returant, in our small town of Augusta, Ky.
 
 
 
 
 
Great trip and really nice to spend time with nephew Arik!
