Maine    8-12-22  
 
12 days with Roger and Bill to Bar Harbor Maine⁄Acadia National Park.
 
2843 miles!   All tent camping except 3 rainy nights in motel.
Gas  2843⁄47 x $4.20          = $254
Camping  8 nights x $15          = 120
Motels  3 x $35                          = 105
Food 12 x 20                              = 240
Misc     100                               = 100
--------------------------------------------------
TOTAL                                      = 819
 
 
8-12-22 Fri  Delayed a day, since we wanted to see Olga one last time (died 5 days later). Met them at McDonald's, Hillslboro.  Camped in Penn near Flight 93 Memorial State Park. Saw bike shop that hosting outside fair event.  Ate at pub in town, where I order pizza.
8-13-22 Sat  Drove to nearby flight 93 monument.  Well done but walked through visitor center quickly and rode to singing monument (wind chimes).
 
Rode back to motorcycle shop to catch Globe of Death motorcycle exhibit.  Pretty darn cool.  In family for 100+ years.  Great photos!
 
Crappy small site, where we used firepit in adjacent site.
 
Stayed at Cooperstown KOA with grassy field and sounds of  coyotes, bull in heat sound, geese, etc.  Pretty cool.
Got New York Pizza in town.  Best we've ever had, crispy crust and all. Lake House resturant snubbed us and wouldn't even acknowledge us so we left after 10 min.
 
Finally arrived at Maine coast.  Now to drive up Rt 1 through all little tourist towns in slow traffic.
 
Motel for $99 with nice resturant⁄bar and played pool for $2⁄game.  I beat Bill with great bankshot, but just barely.
Full hotel but no one at resturant⁄bar.
 
Finally arrived at Maine coast!
 
Acadia National Park Island, where Bar Harbor resides
 
Bill not ready to come back.  He stayed to ride more in this area and up coast, we started journey back.
I wanted lobster meal but due to rain and lateness, never happened!  Embarressing!
 
Top of Mt Washingon, where we paid $25 to ride treacherous rode to top. 
 
Microhotel just before Grand Canyon of PA.  Banging noise aqbove us where Roger used board to bang back at them!!
 
Grand Canyon of PA
 
8-20-22 Stayed at same Cooperstown KOA with grassy field and animal sounds.  Surprisingly, all quiet.  We were looking forward to coyotes, bull min heat sound, geese, etc.  Excited about getting that fantastic New York pizza we had on earlier visit.  Ordered it to go.  1 hour wait.  Picked up and ate their and crust was like shoe leather, not like crispy last time.  Probably sat in togo box for 20 minutes!!!!  Took most back to camp and heated above fire, where now it's super crisp⁄dried out!
 
8-22-22 Last night.  Roger made me birthday meal out of rice⁄bean⁄chicken bag and blueberry crisp bag.  Just add hotwater to both.  Meal was good, crisp was runny!!!
 
Last night.  Rode home next day.
Took off and bike handling off.  Pulled in to gas sttion and gigantic long nail in rear tire!  Plugged and filled with CO2 cartridge to 15psi.  Free air at station to bring to 36.  Both tires worn past tread indicators so it's last trip on them.
Home at 7:30 PM.
 
Great 12 day trip!!
 
Lessons Learned:
- pack a towel  (bought small one)
- pack long sleeve shirt
- pack 2nd pair of jeans  (bought in Walmart)
- don't trust stupid GPS  Watch road signs and ignore GPS for all little shortcuts!
- buy more CO2 bottles
- bring backup phone charging cable  (borrowed Rogers and bought 2)
- bring flip flops for showers
- check tires  (worn to tread indicator)
