New River Gorge, Smokies 8-11-25  (6 days)
 
- Monday 9AM Jim arrived at house and we took off to New River Gorge, where we hiked for 2 miles.
  Clamshell luggage opened up on expressway and dumped my India  backpack bag of 5 days clothes, toiletries, phone charger and power supply!!!  Found Walmart on way and for $133 replaced most of it.  Hiked 1 mile out for overview and back, Sleepin hotel in Sommersville.
 
- Tues Looped up to Snowshoe, stopped at a small historic museum⁄confederate cemetary where we took a picture and walked around the beautiful 2 story house that was beginning to fall apart and museum closed.  Nice break though.   I took a tight turn and rear tire slid but caught again.  Jim said he smelled burning rubber!!! Greenbrier hotel in White Sulfur Springs, Sleepin in Beckley (fair in Springs where all hotels doubled$$).  Shoneys for dinner which was Senior night special on buffett!  Pretty darn good for $13 and $7 tip!
 
- Wed Solid rain, we took expressways to Maggie Valley Somewhere through Time museum ,closed!! Sleepin in Bryson which had an outside pool.
  Swimsuit lost ,so I just went in my shorts.
 
- Thurs Back to Museum. Road delayed so we rerouted and took some of the Blueridge Parkway to get there.  Spent 2 hours there.  Hosts walked us through and started some bikes up.  Pretty darn cool.
   Lunch at Deals Gap. bought T-shirt that I lost, rode Dragon and headed home.  Stuck on I75 for 1⁄2 hour creeping at 2 mph due to trailor slight accident and rubber neckers..
   Started pouring rain at 4PM, we pulled off. I said I'm checking into hotel, Jim going to keep going once it blows over.  He got home at 8.  I checked
   into $73 Baymont inn.
 
- Fri Headed for Natural Bridge campsite.  Walmart for fruit⁄sandwhich, yogurt, bluetooth speaker and power supply for USB stuff, couple of USB cables also.Only walkins avail which worked. 2 orders of wood and I'm set.  Order of wings at lodge bar and (2) $7 draft beers.
 
- Sat  Headed home the backway after going through Nada tunnel.  Home by 1 PM.  I'm tired!
Great trip!!!!
