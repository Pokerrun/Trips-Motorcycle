Rt 66 Ozarks, Mo 7-29-24  Jim and I, on a 5-day Motorcycle trip.
92-95 degrees each day, but humidity felt low and not bad at all.
 
Mon.  Met Jim at his house at 7AM.  74 to Indy, 70 to St Louis.  Then got on iconic Rt 66.
Toured Merremac Caverns,  $26.
Had reservations at iconic Wagon Wheel Hotel, Cuba Mo. $63  Small but nice.
Walked across lot to famous BarBQ resturant, which was great and low priced.
 
Tues.  Down 66 and saw many of Cuba's murals on walls, which they are known for. 
Saw World's largest rocking chair.  Road lacking signs and zig zags across I44, so it's real easy to get off.
Toured Rolla, Jim's home town. Stopped in at barbershop, which was adjacent to his fathers clothing store.  100 years and Jim still remembers high chair as a kid he sat on!  In library and he reminisced with older desk lady. Maidrite fast food diner before we left.
Found the Devils Elbow famous bridge landmark.... nothing too much at this time.
Stopped at Uranus Fudge Factory, which is a little tourist shopping area.
Finally arrived in Lebanon and stayed at another Iconic hotel,   .  In it's day, it was probably great but my room was neglected but bedding was clean.  Went to Rt66 museum at the local library, which was free and pretty interesting.  Came back, walked across the street to bowling alley, where we just had a beer and each a small pizza.
 
Wed.  Drove south to and met Jim's YouTube biker at Manchester, John xxxx.  Spoke for 20 minutes, giving us good routes but he couldn't join us.  Drove up scenic route 19 and finally stopped at a very nice hotel in Salem, where she even gave me the military discount of $12, for a total of only $123.  Includes a nice breakfast.  I used pool⁄hot tub.  Walked to Mexican restaurant.  Unfortunately, Jim cold caused him to only get 1 hour sleep!
 
Thurs.  Drove to highest point in Mo, Taum Sauk Mountain, which included 2 miles of gravel road to get there.  Not much of a view, surprisingly.  Destination of Metropolis, Il superman's home!  Phone map zig-zagged wildly to get there, when NO highways was selected. Pics with superman, Jim bought a small figurine for his friend and ate at cheap nice restaurant in town.  Took off for home with rain coming.  Finally pulled over, Madisonville Ky.  Holiday Inn and Hampton wanted $198 before taxes.  Found small Indian owned for $64 total.  Rooms nice and clean.
 
Fri  Headed for home.  Arbys for lunch.  Home by 2:30.

Great Trip!
