Hazard Ky 10-21-22 with Nephew Arik
Arik rode his Sportster from Chicago and arrived Thurs at 7PM.
Gave him Brake Free Stop light as a safety gift.
Fri 9AM, temps in upper 40's, we took off.
565 total miles for me.  Arik has another 700!
 
 
Ferry across Ohio river to to Rt52.
10 miles and we stopped at Rankin house in Ripley.
Crossed back to KY and took Rt8 backway to covered bridge.
Rt8 to Rt23, Country Music Highway.  Ton of stop lights on Rt23.  Eric said lets find Loretta Lynn's Birthplace home, which was just off the road.
GPS stopped us early at old yellow house with no markings.  Kept going and found the real thing Nam said watch out for the donkey, so we left quickly.
Finally made it to our hotel.  5:30 and room not cleaned yet.  Went had dinner.  Came back and neither card worked! 
 
Sat 7AM departure to Kingdom Come State Park, to get some mountain views.
Cold out.  Stopped at gas station after 15 miles for coffee to warm up. Great twisty roads and views.  Park had great views.
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
Headed for NADA tunnel.   Stopped for lunch at Hazard Diner.
GPS stopped us short at NADA tunnel.  After going in circles we finally continued on and went through tunnel.
Cruised the NAtural Bridge State Park a little, but not much to see unless you want to walk to Natural Bridge.  Headed home.
Stopped at Blue Lick Battlefields State Park, specifically to see Battlefield.  Found cemetary and monument but no markd battlefield.  Went into gift shop and got a few pamphlets.... Revolutionary War, not Civil
Traded bikes  His is a Harley allright, loud and rough.  Ran great and fun.  Legs outstretched!
Stopped at Autozone to get a headlamp...bright turns both off. 
Home at 7:30   Great trip!
 
Pub for dinner and across street and talked with Noah!  Cool.
Signed up for Netflix to watch THe Deer Hunter, but they don't have it!  Bought Deer Hunter on Prime for $17!  Arik fell asleep which is smart!.
 
Sun  Pumped up FZR1000 tire and bled air out of clutch lines so Aril could take it for a spin.  Loved it and said it was FAST!
 
Gave him my 31 year old jacket and Winter gloves.  Checked his oil which was not hitting dipstick!
Arik left and texted he made it home by 8:30 our time.   Quite a smart got-his-shit-together young man!  Hope we do this next year!
