Kingdom Come State Park⁄Chattanooga 4-23-25  (3 days)
 
Met Jim at Gen Store and rode a little past Morehead.  He then split off to go home and I continued to Kingdom Come State Park to camp.   Hiked up to see a rock structure.  Problem getting wifi, but could get if I hiked up to the wooden meeting structure.
 
 
 
Took off South to Chatannooga, thinking maybe I'll go all the way to the Gulf.  Passed the Coal Creek Miners Museum in Rocky Top TN, which I stopped at.  I was only visitor and Debby walked me through the exhibits, starting with a 10 min movie.  1902 tragic explosion killing around 200 men⁄children.  Bought a souvenier coffee cup and donated $18 to their  museum.  Got barbecue sandwhich next door.  Finally stopped at Home Town Inn Soddy-Daisy, TN  hotel, since it was raining and not in mood for tenting.  $82 vs 40 for tent.  Nice clean hotel.  Food Store and gought a salad, apple, bannanna abd yogurt for dinner. 
Gulf would be 431 miles where Augusta is only 339.  Back next day with solid rain the whole day, luckily it was warm out and rainsuit kept me very dry.  Expressways back.
 
Good 3 days.
