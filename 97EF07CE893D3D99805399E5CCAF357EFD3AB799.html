North Bend⁄Twin Falls WV 10-13-25  Roger⁄Bill⁄Tom   3 nights
- 10-13  Mon  Met at 41⁄32 McDonalds.  32⁄50 to North Bend State Park, WV  203 miles.
Nice 2 bedroon cabin.  No firepit.  Roger and I had dinner in Lodge.  Youtube video on bikes including highlites of my India trip video.
- 10-14 Tues Breakfast at lodge then rode down 16 and loop back.  Late lunch in town. Watched more Youtube bike videos.
- 10-15 Wed Checked out and drove down 16 to Beckley, where we stayed at Twin Falls State Park.  Biscuit World for late lunch and to go orders for dinner.
Nice fire pit with ton of free wood makes enjoyable night sipping beer and sitting around fire,
- 10-16 Thurs Took 52N to get home. I split off at Ky⁄Oh border and took Rt8 backway home.  Home at 5:30 PM. 
Except for Rogers light going out, I thought I lost my wallet when I tried to pay at gas station.  Unbelievably it dropped out of my MC outer pants onto ground!!!  I thought for sure I lost it 100 mile North.....praise the Lord!  Great 4 day relaxing good weather trip.
